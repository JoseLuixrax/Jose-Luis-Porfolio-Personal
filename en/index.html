About me

A technical profile between business, data and code

I'm a backend developer and the technical lead at VapoSelección, an e-commerce business that sells through its online store, on Amazon (Spain, France, Germany and Italy) and in three physical stores in the province of Córdoba.

I make sure the digital side of the business works: I migrated the infrastructure from shared hosting to our own VPS with Docker, I maintain and develop the PrestaShop store, I integrate Amazon SP-API and Odoo, and I automate repetitive processes with n8n and Python.

I studied Web Application Development and then completed a Vocational Master's in Artificial Intelligence and Big Data. That lets me move comfortably between product code, data and applied AI.

I work pragmatically: understand the business process, measure it and build the simplest solution that solves it reliably, then document it so it doesn't depend on a single person.

What I'm looking for

A team where I can contribute in backend, integrations, automation or e-commerce platforms, with technical challenges and room to improve processes from start to finish.

How I work

  • I understand the business problem before writing code, and I take it all the way to production.
  • If a task keeps repeating, it gets automated.
  • If something fails, it should say so and explain what happened.
  • Enough documentation for others to maintain it.
